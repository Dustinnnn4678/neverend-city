Privacy

Draft. This page is a plain-language first draft and has not been reviewed by a lawyer yet.

Right now

The public city page collects nothing about you. It only reads public follower and subscriber counts. If you turn on the demo mode, everything stays in your own browser.

When accounts are switched on

  • What we keep: your email (only to sign you in), your points, streak, claimed building and cosmetics, and whether you confirmed you are 13 or older.
  • What is public: only a username you chose to show. Your email is never shown to anyone.
  • Why: to run the game, stop cheating, and run fair giveaways.
  • Who sees it: our database host (Supabase) and email sender. We do not sell data.
  • Delete it: in My hub → Privacy you can delete your account and all your data at any time.
