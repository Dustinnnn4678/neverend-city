# Sponsor Terms (DRAFT, needs legal review)

1. **Requests.** Businesses and creators may request a branded store, billboard or landmark. Pricing depends on size, location and duration (for example billboard 30 days, storefront 90 days, landmark 1 year).
2. **Approval.** Every request goes to the operator for approval before anything appears. The operator may reject or remove any content, at any time, for any reason.
3. **Labeling.** All sponsored items are labeled "Sponsored" in the city.
4. **Content rules.** No illegal, deceptive, hateful, adult, political-campaign, gambling, or tobacco/vape content. You confirm you own or have permission for all logos and text you submit.
5. **Reporting.** We provide simple anonymous view counts (how many visitors saw your item). We do not promise a number of views.
6. **Payment and duration.** Payment is taken at request. If rejected, you are refunded in full. The booking runs for the chosen duration starting at approval.
7. **Ending.** After the term your item is removed unless renewed.
8. **No effect on counts.** Sponsored items never change the follower/subscriber count or any ranking.
