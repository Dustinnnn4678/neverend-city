Help and FAQ

Neverend City grows by one building for every Instagram follower and YouTube subscriber.

Getting started

How do I get a building?

Open the city, tap Quests → My building, create a free account, pick any building, and type your Instagram or YouTube name. It takes about 10 seconds.

Do I need to be 13 or older?

Yes. You confirm this when you create your account. Under 13s cannot use the accounts part of the site.

What is the check mark on my name plate?

It means you proved the account is yours by commenting your code on the latest post or video. You also get +100 ★.

I forgot my password.

On the log-in screen press Forgot your password? and we will email you a reset link. Check your spam folder too.

Stars, coins and rewards

What are Stars (★)?

Stars are your progress points. You earn them by visiting every day, finding golden stars, finishing quests and more. They level you up and buy cosmetics for your building.

What are City Coins?

Coins run your home and business inside your building. They are earned by running a business and are never sold, traded or cashed out.

Does paying for anything change the follower count or leaderboards?

No. Supporting the city only gives thank-you extras like a badge. It never changes counts, levels or leaderboards.

Are crates gambling?

Crates can only be earned by playing, never bought, and their odds are shown to everyone.

Problems

Something looks wrong or is slow.

Open the ⚙ menu on the city and try Graphics: Low. If it is still wrong, press Report a bug there (sign in first) and we get your device details automatically.

Someone broke the rules.

Tap Quests → More → Report a problem. Reports go to a moderator.

Can I install it like an app?

Yes. On iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android or desktop Chrome: menu → Install app.

How do I delete my account?

Quests → More → Delete my account and data. This removes everything and cannot be undone.

Contact

The fastest way is the in-site Report a bug button. For anything else, send a direct message to the Neverend City account on Instagram or YouTube.
